# Blindspot pass

Use when the user says they lack working knowledge of a territory, or two replies in a row show they cannot weigh a question that needs domain judgment. An undecided user is not a trigger.

Before the first question in that territory, ask once: **Map the territory first** or **Continue with questions**. Declining the map does not authorize a default.

The map is a decision map, not a tutorial. Ground it in the relevant code, configuration, and schemas, or in available sources for an outside territory; label anything else as unverified model knowledge. Show facts already settled by evidence with their source, not as choices. List only the decisions that need the user's judgment, each in their vocabulary with why it matters, the viable options, and a recommendation, ordered by effect on the outcome. A hazard states its impact and becomes a decision only when its mitigations need a choice.

Then ask the decisions in rounds as `interview-me` does, and record settled answers, assumptions, and hazards in the brainstorm summary. The pass runs once per territory.
